# How To
This page gives you some ideas for some of the things you can do in Galacticraft.

## How to start
* The most essential machine is the [Ingot Compressor](/wiki/machines/compressor.md), this is needed to make compressed metals which are used throughout Galacticraft
* Most people will want power, so you will need either [Coal Generator](/wiki/machines/power/coal_generator.md) or [Solar Panel](/wiki/machines/power/advanced_solar_panel.md) - and to make a Solar Panel you need a [Circuit Fabricator](/wiki/machines/circuit_fabricator.md)
* You should mine plenty of iron, coal, copper, tin and aluminum, as well as some silicon and diamonds
* [Compressed Steel](/wiki/items/manufactured/compressed_steel.md) is a very useful ingredient - it can be used to make [Heavy Duty Armor](/wiki/items/armor/heavy_duty_chestplate.md) which is almost as good as diamond armor, for less cost. [Heavy Duty Pickaxe](/wiki/items/tools/heavy_duty_pickaxe.md) has the same harvest level as a diamond pickaxe, it can mine Obsidian.
* Some people want to rush towards building their first [rocket](/wiki/entities/vehicles/tier_1_rocket.md), this will need plenty of metals
* Alternatively you could build up a broader base or control center on the Overworld, with [power](/meta/concepts/general/energy.md) and automated machines, [pipes](/wiki/machines/oxygen/fluid_pipe.md) and [tanks](/wiki/machines/oxygen/fluid_tank.md), [Hydraulic Platforms](), [Magnetic Crafting Tables](), [Display Screens](), [Communications Dish](), [Lighting Panels]() and other technological elements
* A [Standard Wrench](/wiki/items/tools/special/standard_wrench.md) is always useful for arranging blocks and pipes to your liking.

# Galacticraft 3
**Galacticraft 3** (GC3) is the Minecraft 1.7.10 update to the Galacticraft mod series. It is included in many modpacks for Minecraft 1.7.10.

The main additions in GC3 include the [Asteroids](/wiki/dimensions/celestial_bodies/asteroids.md) dimension, the [Tier 3 Rocket](/wiki/entities/vehicles/tier_3_rocket.md), freefall motion outside Space Stations, rotating [space stations](/wiki/dimensions/space_stations/overworld_space_station.md), the introduction of a temperature system, [Titanium Armor](/meta/concepts/player/titanium_armor.md), [Titanium Tools](/meta/concepts/player/titanium_tools.md), a second tier to the power system, [Display Screens](/wiki/machines/space_base/display_screen.md), and the [Astro Miner](/wiki/entities/vehicles/astro_miner.md). There are many other new tools, machines, and items, including more ways to make Oxygen and Fuel, and various other [Gases](/meta/concepts/general/gases.md#galacticraft-3) which can be liquefied. Finally, GC3 adds smoother gameplay and expanded functions and abilities for some existing items to add even more to your experience in outer space!

Galacticraft 3 was officially released on November 9, 2014. It has been continually updated and added to since that time, and all players are strongly recommended to update to the **Latest** version (as of early 2017, that's version 3.0.12.485 or later). Even if you are playing in a modpack, it's OK to download newer Galacticraft files and replace the ones used by the modpack. But if playing on a server, make sure you match the server version!

Galacticraft 3 was first released in Beta form on July 24, 2014 for Minecraft 1.7.2, and on July 31, 2014 for Minecraft 1.7.10 / 1.7.2 (dual compatible).

Galacticraft 3 consists of three mod files: **GalacticraftCore**, **Galacticraft-Planets** and **MicdoodleCore**. Galacticraft-Planets was formerly named GalacticraftMars, and that is still its internal mod name.

## Gameplay Improvements
* Shift-click oxygen gear, oxygen tanks and other Galacticraft items into and out of [inventory slots](/meta/concepts/player/player_inventory_tab.md)
* Shift-click works in all machines
* Infinite Oxygen item for easy base setup in Creative mode
* The place where you first land on a planet becomes your spawn point on that world
* The player can breathe when inside a rocket or lander
* You cannot exit a rocket using 'Shift' after it has launched
* You cannot exit a lander using 'Shift' until it has properly landed
* Using [Launch Controllers](/wiki/machines/pads/launch_controller.md) you can fly in a rocket between two pads in the same dimension
* Creative mode Galacticraft tabs reorganized into 2 tabs
* All machines and Solar Panels are now 'Enabled' by default when first placed
* Player model stays properly inside the rocket if you tilt the rocket
* /dimensiontp command with no player name teleports yourself

# Rocket

The **Rockets** are arguably the most vital part of [Galacticraft](/README.md).

**Rocket** may refer to:
* [Tier 1 Rocket](/entities/vehicles/tier_1_rocket.md) - the first rocket you can build. It will bring you to the [Moon](/dimensions/celestial_bodies/moon.md) and [Overworld Space Station](/dimensions/space_stations/overworld_space_station.md).
* [Tier 2 Rocket](/entities/vehicles/tier_2_rocket.md) - This can be built after you have looted the [Moon Dungeon](/structures/dungeons/moon_dungeon.md) for the [Tier 2 Rocket Schematic](/items/schematics/tier_2_rocket_schematic.md). Brings you to all tier 1 dimensions as well as [Mars](/dimensions/celestial_bodies/mars.md).
* [Tier 3 Rocket](/entities/vehicles/tier_3_rocket.md) - This can be built after you have looted the [Mars Dungeon](/structures/dungeons/mars_dungeon.md) for the [Tier 3 Rocket Schematic](/items/schematics/tier_3_rocket_schematic.md). Brings you to all tier 1 and 2 dimensions, as well as [Asteroids](/dimensions/celestial_bodies/asteroids.md) and [Venus](/dimensions/celestial_bodies/venus.md).
* [Cargo Rocket](/entities/vehicles/cargo_rocket.md) - A rocket that is able to transport a large payload automatically. Can only be built after you have looted the [Mars Dungeon](/structures/dungeons/mars_dungeon.md) for the [Cargo Rocket Schematic](/items/schematics/cargo_rocket_schematic.md).
* [Tier 4 Rocket](/entities/vehicles/tier_4_rocket.md) - This rocket was added by the "More Planets" add-on. Brings you to all tiers 1, 2, and 3 dimensions, as well as Diona, Mercury, and Jupiter.
* Tier 5 Rocket - This rocket was added by the "More Planets" add-on. Brings you to all tiers 1, 2, 3, and 4 dimensions, as well as Dark Asteroids, Pluto, and Polongnius.

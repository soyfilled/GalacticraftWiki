# Sun
The **Sun**, also known as *Sol*, is a [celestial body](./) that governs the day-night cycle of all dimensions of the [Galacticraft](/README.md) mod, including the [Overworld](./overworld.md), and can be seen in the sky of all [dimensions](/wiki/dimensions). All the main planets and the [asteroid belts](/wiki/dimensions/celestial_bodies/asteroids.md) orbit it.

In spite of its importance, this celestial body is not a dimension, therefore it is not accessible nor is it land-able, and will not ever be.

See [Sun](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sun) at the official Minecraft Wiki.

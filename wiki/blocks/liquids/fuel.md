# Fuel
![Fuel](/img/blocks/liquids/fuel/Fuel.png)      
![Fuel (Item)](/img/blocks/liquids/fuel/Grid_Fuel.png)

**Fuel** is vital to running your [Spaceship](/meta/common/rocket.md). It is also used to fuel the [Moon Buggy](/wiki/entities/vehicles/moon_buggy.md).

## Obtaining
There are two ways of obtaining fuel.

### Oil
For this method, you will need to find some [Crude Oil](crude_oil.md). Use a bucket to gather it and place it in a [Refinery](/wiki/machines/refinery.md), which will convert the oil into fuel. You can store up to 24000 units of fuel in the refinery. You can then pump it into a [Fuel Loader](/wiki/machines/pads/fuel_loader.md), [Fluid Tank](/wiki/machines/oxygen/fluid_tank.md) or place an [Empty Canister](/wiki/items/liquids_gases/empty_canister.md) in the output slot.

### Methane
Note: this method requires [Meteoric Iron Ingots](/wiki/items/materials/meteoric_iron_ingot.md), which is only found on [asteroids](/wiki/dimensions/celestial_bodies/asteroids.md). Therefore, the oil method must be used first.

For this method, use a [Water Electrolyzer](/wiki/machines/water_electrolyzer.md) to gather [Hydrogen](/meta/concepts/general/gases.md#hydrogen), which can then be combined with [Fragmented Carbon](/wiki/items/materials/fragmented_carbon.md) in a [Methane Synthesizer](/wiki/machines/methane_synthesizer.md) to make [Methane Gas](/meta/concepts/general/gases.md#methane). This can then be used in a [Gas Liquefier](/wiki/machines/gas_liquefier.md) to make rocket fuel. Again, this can be pumped into a fuel loader, fluid tank or empty canister.

## How to Use Rocket Fuel
In order to launch the Rocket, you must place a [Fuel Loader](/wiki/machines/pads/fuel_loader.md) next to a [Rocket Launch Pad](/wiki/machines/pads/rocket_launch_pad.md). Place a filled [Fuel Canister](/wiki/items/liquids_gases/fuel_canister.md) into the left hand slot in the Fuel Loader, which will automatically transfer its fuel into the Fuel Loader's internal tank. Give the Fuel Loader some power by inserting a charged [Battery](/wiki/items/manufactured/battery.md) or connecting up with [Aluminum Wire](/wiki/machines/power/aluminum_wire.md). If done correctly, the Fuel will start loading into the [Spaceship](/meta/common/rocket.md). After the Fuel has fully loaded into the Spaceship, the player can launch. If you are planning to go to the [Moon](/wiki/dimensions/celestial_bodies/moon.md) make sure to give the Spaceship plenty of fuel, because you will need enough to come home again: see [Pre-flight Checklist](/meta/tutorials/pre-flight_checklist.md).

## Warning: highly flammable!
If you have a liquid block of Fuel in your game world, be careful: if there is any fire near it, it can cause an explosion. (Feature added in Galacticraft version 3.0.8.265)
